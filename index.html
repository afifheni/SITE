About Iblea Fresh Export

Based in Marina di Ragusa, Sicily, Iblea Fresh Export develops sourcing connections between Northern European buyers and selected Sicilian fresh produce suppliers.

With direct field experience in greenhouse operations and packaging activities in the Ragusa area, we understand the importance of quality, reliability and efficient coordination.
